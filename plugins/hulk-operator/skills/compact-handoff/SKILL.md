---
name: compact-handoff
description: Create compact HULK checkpoints so Chat, Work and Codex can resume without replaying long history.
---

At meaningful milestones, capture only:
- objective/current state;
- exact files or systems changed;
- validations/tests completed;
- unresolved blocker;
- next action;
- decisions that must remain fixed.

Do not paste full conversation history or unchanged documents when a compact handoff is enough.
